About me

I am Rachel Meier and I am an undergraduate student attending Texas Tech University. As of Spring 2026, I am senior in the Computer Science department where I am learning about the foundations of coding languages, applications of computer science and technology in multiple fields, and teamwork skills regarding projects and communication.

Before attending Texas Tech University, I completed an associate in general studies At South Plains College with a GPA above 3.5. At Tech, I have kept a GPA above 3.0 and aim to research where I would like to specialize in my current career path.

Due to the broad range of applications present within the computer science field, I have not specialized in a research area, but some concepts that have intrigued me are data centers, data analysis and management, and LiDAR function and application. Discovery has been a defining part of leading me to this field with something always new to learn. While my logical nature applies to my work and relationship skills, the deep curiosity of the constant evolution of technology drives me forward.

Being a commuter means I am not as deeply involved with extracurricular aspects of Texas Tech campus, but I am much more familiar with Lubbock itself. When out of school I enjoy attending the musical and theater productions run by LCU, and developing a proper work-life balance by spending time with my friends and loved ones. With how fast my mind runs, I find myself taking up dexterous hobbies like crochet and embroidery, and hope to become skilled enough to share those projects with those in need.
